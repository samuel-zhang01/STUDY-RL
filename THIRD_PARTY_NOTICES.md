STUDY-RL is an educational collection of original notes, exercises, synthetic
fixtures, and reference implementations. The repository history credits
Samuel Zhang (`samuel-zhang01`) and Guglielmo Cimolai for its development.

The repository license covers contributions for which the contributors hold
the necessary rights. It does not relicense cited research papers, linked
websites, external libraries, pretrained model weights, datasets downloaded
separately, or their trademarks. Those remain under their respective licenses
and terms. Research papers are linked and cited rather than bundled as copies.

The optional lab defaults to `HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M-Instruct`. Consult its
model card before downloading or redistributing weights. Generated adapters
and downloaded model weights are excluded from the repository.

Insurance, recruitment, and patient examples in the bundled projects use
locally generated synthetic data. The ATS architecture is a hypothetical
teaching design. It is not an implementation or audit of a private production
system. Public research and event references in the causal-biology lessons
are identified in the [source dossier](research/causal_biology.md).
